### Fixed
- Read the plan of a Yahoo fund name with an abbreviation between the plan and IDCW, such as "Dir Mn IDCW-R", "Dir Qt IDCW-P" or "Reg Wk IDCW-R", so a search result or confirmed fund on another plan than the statement's gets its warning badge. Before, such a name got only an IDCW badge, and no plan warning. The add-on now reads Yahoo's abbreviations of daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, bi-monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, periodic, normal, flexi, standard and discretionary IDCW, payment, bonus, others and lock-in; "Rg" and "Dr" for the plan; "IDCWP", "IDCWR" and "IDCW-T"; and Yahoo names whose words lost their spaces, such as "Reg MthIDCW-P" or "DirGr". A statement's name that says Normal, Flexi, Standard, Discretionary or Annually IDCW, IDCW Others, Lock-in or Principal Units now gives its plan too
- Follow a holdings import's record of a folio, demat account or PRAN to its asset after the asset's symbol or exchange changes in Wealthfolio, whatever the holding's remembered choice: confirmed again since the change, left out on the Holdings card, without an asset ID, or confirmed from a search result that gave another symbol or exchange than the asset's profile. Imports now record each holding's Wealthfolio asset ID. A statement of that folio, demat account or PRAN that no longer lists the holding then removes it by default, with the note "Not on the statement any more". Another asset that now has the recorded symbol and exchange doesn't take over the record, even when the recorded asset is no longer in the account. For an asset the import's save creates or finds, the add-on reads its ID from the saved snapshot; if it can't, it records the holding without one and says some import history could not be saved. A record that version 2.1.0 or earlier saved has no asset IDs until an import keeps or imports its holdings, and one that such a version saves on another synced device can lose them: it follows the asset only if the holding's remembered choice is that asset, confirmed while it had the recorded symbol and exchange. Before, without such a choice, the preview could treat the asset like a holding no import added, keeping it by default without the note, and saving a kept holding dropped its record
- Ask in a holdings import whether to keep or remove a holding that an earlier import of a folio, demat account or PRAN added to an account, once the statement puts that folio, demat account or PRAN in another account. Before, the old account kept the holding by default with no note while the new account imported the same units, and still kept them once the folio no longer listed them. Now the old account's row names the folio, demat account or PRAN, says which account it goes to and whether it still lists the holding, and saving waits for the choice; remove the units only if all of them came from it, or from the old account's own folios, demat accounts or PRANs that recorded the holding too and no longer list it, which before removed it by default. When a left-out or unlisted folio, demat account or PRAN may have added some of the units too, or the identifiers can't be compared, the holding is kept with a note to correct its units in Wealthfolio, and an old asset isn't offered for replacement, nor a fund left out for overlap for removal. The statement's rows in the old account stay as they were: the folios that stay there replace its units with theirs. Once the import replaces or removes a holding, the moved folio, demat account or PRAN's record in the old account no longer lists it, so later imports don't ask again. A folio that shares one record with a folio that stays, linked through the issuer's folio number, still lists what that one imported
- Tell apart two fund houses' folios in an NSDL or CDSL statement that have the same folio number, or that both have none. Each such folio's label now adds the first fund printed under it and how many other funds the folio has, such as "Folio 91234567/12 (Fictiva Flexi Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth and 1 other fund)", and the fund's ISIN too when the first funds' names read the same or the completed labels still collide. Before, both read "Folio <number>", or "Mutual fund folios", on the Accounts card, in the Left out alert, the snapshot preview and Check units, and a note about both named only one of them. Other folios keep their labels, and remembered account choices and import records still apply

## [2.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added
- Show each fund's plan (Direct or Regular) and option (Growth or IDCW) as badges beside its name on the statement, its search results and the confirmed fund, on the Funds and Holdings cards, to make names like "DIRECT PLAN GROWTH" and "Dir Gr" easier to compare. The add-on reads them from the end of each name; a result's or confirmed fund's badge is a warning when it differs from the statement's, and a name that doesn't give its plan or option has no badge for it
- Import `GIFT_IN` and `GIFT_OUT` as unit transfers at the gift day's NAV, with no cash. A gift received on its own starts its cost and holding period that day; a matched pair in two accounts carries the giver's recorded costs and purchase dates. The preview leaves out same-account pairs and unsafe or inconsistent rows, and flags likely earlier entries. See the README's Gifts section for pairing rules and the tax-cost distinction
- Warn about a search result or a confirmed fund or holding quoted in a currency other than rupees, such as a US listing found by a company's name. On the Funds and Holdings cards, its currency is a warning badge beside it instead of the last item in the grey "(…)" details, with the line "The statement's prices and amounts are in rupees, but this asset is quoted in USD. Check that it's the right listing." The detailed preview says the same on each of the fund's transactions, and the snapshot preview on the holding's row, naming the asset's symbol. It stays a warning: such a listing can still be confirmed, previewed and imported. Wealthfolio values the units at the chosen listing's price, in its currency, while the add-on sends the statement's prices and amounts in rupees. Before, only the currency at the end of the grey details told it apart

### Changed
- Ask on the Funds card to check that each fund found matches the statement's name, plan and option, then choose Use this fund, which is needed only the first time. The "No AMFI result" paragraph repeated under each fund, which read as a problem with that fund, is now one note below the Funds or Holdings card's introduction, shown while a fund's search finds no AMFI result: it recommends AMFI for daily fund prices, added in Settings → Market data before confirming, and says to search each fund's ISIN or AMFI scheme code
- Read statements with pypdfium2 5.15.2, up from 5.14.0. It's built on PDFium 159.0.8149.0, up from 156.0.8076.0, which pypdfium2's release notes say fixes a few CVEs. 5.15.2 also fixes a rare error when closing a statement after reading it, if Python's garbage collector ran at the same time
- Read a statement PDF that has no password, such as one saved again from a PDF viewer, with the PDF password box left empty: Read statement needs only the file, and a note under the box says it can stay empty. Before, it stayed disabled until something was typed. A statement that has a password still says "Wrong password" when read without one. With Remember the password ticked, an empty password isn't saved, and a saved password stays as it is
- License the add-on under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or any later version (AGPL-3.0-or-later), like Wealthfolio's AGPL-3.0, instead of the MIT License. Releases through 2.0.1 stay under the MIT License, and the components the package carries keep their own licences, listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
- Say in a holdings import when an earlier import added a holding from a folio, demat account or PRAN that the statement doesn't list. Its row is still kept by default, since a statement can leave out a folio for other reasons, such as one requested for some fund houses only, and now says "An earlier import added it from a folio, demat account or PRAN that this statement doesn't list. Check whether it was redeemed, sold or moved, and untick Keep if so." A holding that the statement's own folios, demat accounts or PRANs no longer list is kept the same way, with a note, instead of removed. An old asset such an import added isn't offered for replacement by the asset its fund is now confirmed to, and a fund left out for overlap doesn't offer to remove the account's units, since they may include that folio's. Once an import replaces or removes the holding, that folio's import record no longer lists it. To find these records, the add-on keeps an index for each account of the folios, demat accounts and PRANs that each investor's imports put there, hashed like the records, so only a statement of the same investor finds them, and a folio last imported by version 2.0.1 or earlier is found only once a statement lists it again. Concurrent device imports can overwrite index additions because storage can't merge them; a lost entry gives no note or protection from removal until a later import lists its source again, and the other snapshot rules apply. Before, such a holding was kept with only "Not on the statement", like one added by hand, so a redeemed fund couldn't be told from one the statement doesn't cover

### Fixed
- Say when casparser couldn't read a statement's date or period, as soon as the statement is read: "casparser couldn't read this statement's date or period, so it can't be imported." casparser leaves the period empty when it doesn't recognise where a statement prints it. Before, a detailed statement was read anyway, and only after its accounts were chosen and every fund confirmed did each Preview the import fail with "Couldn't prepare the preview: Error: Not a statement date: ". A summary or depository statement showed "Couldn't read the statement" with only "Not a statement date: "
- Leave out a summary statement's line whose units × NAV don't give its value, to the paisa plus the units' and NAV's rounding, or that has units but no NAV, or a value but no units, and say it may have been misread and needs checking against the PDF. casparser 1.4.1 reads a cell it can't read as 0, and on made-up statements an amount of ₹1 crore or more without its leading digit, without a warning. Before, a cost of ₹1 crore or more could be saved without its leading digit, a fund whose units couldn't be read could be removed as if it had been sold, and one whose NAV couldn't be read got a price of ₹0. The holdings import now leaves that fund's holding in the folio's account as it is, with its units, cost, price and what earlier imports added, or doesn't add it, even from another folio's line; Check units marks it for review
- Don't count a holding twice in a holdings import when it's confirmed to another asset than the one an earlier import of its folio, demat account or PRAN added, such as AMFI's result after Yahoo's, or a summary statement's fund priced from statements that now prints its ISIN. The preview keeps the old asset and leaves the new one out, and the new one's row offers to replace the old asset with it, naming both. Imports record identifiers, not units, so when the old asset may also hold other units, or a possibly misread line holds it back, both rows say so and ask to correct its units in Wealthfolio if both are kept. Before, the old asset kept its old units and the new one got the statement's, so the account held the fund twice
- Ask whether to keep or remove a holdings account's units of a folio's fund that the preview leaves out because an account that tracks transactions holds it, when an earlier import of that folio recorded adding the holding for its own lines alone. The row now says leaving it out keeps the account's units unless they are removed, so keeping them counts them in both accounts, and saving waits for the choice; removing them confirms that all of them came from that folio, since imports don't record how many units each folio, demat account or PRAN added. An old asset that an earlier import of the folio added for the fund, now confirmed to another asset left out for overlap, gets the same choice instead of being kept by default. If its units may include others, or no earlier import of that folio recorded the holding, they are kept with a note that both accounts may count them and to check them in Wealthfolio. Before, the left-out row kept the account's units without a word, and the preview had no way to take them out
- Never save a statement's price that isn't positive for a holding priced by hand, including a price another account's import supplies for a kept holding, which then gets back the price it had
- Use the singular for a count of one in the detailed import's preview and result, and in the line that describes a statement once it's read: "1 new transaction", "1 possible duplicate", "Import 1 activity", "Imported 1 of 1 activity", "skipped 1 as a duplicate and created 1 fund", "Linked 1 of 1 bank row you chose with its transaction", "1 folio, 1 scheme, 1 row" and "1 demat account, 1 folio, 1 holding". Before, these counts were always plural, as in "1 new transactions" and "Import 1 activities"
- Use the singular for a count of one in the warning for a merger left out because its other half can't be matched, as in "the folio has 1 merger sale and 0 merger purchases on this day". Before, it read "1 merger sales and 0 merger purchases"
- Tell screen readers which fund, holding or transaction each repeated control is for, with hidden text after its visible text, which stays as it was. On the Funds and Holdings cards: "Search for Fictiva Flexi Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth", for the search box and its button, "Use this fund for …", "Use the statement's prices for …", "Leave this fund out for …", "Change the fund for …" and "Choose a fund for …", with "share", "bond" or "NPS scheme" for other holdings, and "Search results for …" for the list of results. In the detailed preview: "Import anyway for the 2023-05-05 purchase of …, 25000.00, folio …, transaction …", "Use it instead of a new transfer out for the …" and "Import this fund anyway for …, folio …". In the snapshot preview: "Import … in …" or "Keep … in …" for each holding, and "Total cost of … in …", naming the account, and "Replace the snapshot of 2025-03-31 in Demat". In Check units: "Same holdings for …". Equal holding or account names get a number to tell their controls apart, including separate overlapping contributions to one snapshot holding. The closing units table after an import also gets a Status header over its Match and Differ column, which had none. Before, every copy of a control had the same name, such as "Search for the fund", "Import anyway" or "Same holdings", so a screen reader user moving between controls heard the same name for each fund or row
- Keep the keyboard focus on the page when a control replaces itself. On the Funds and Holdings cards, the control that takes a pressed one's place in the fund's section gets the focus: Change once Use this fund or Use the statement's prices confirms a fund, Choose a fund after Leave this fund out, and the search box after Change or Choose a fund. Import N activities moves the focus to the result's first message, such as "Imported 23 of 23 activities", and Save N snapshots to "Saved 2 snapshots", or to "Wealthfolio didn't save these snapshots" if none saved. Import this fund anyway moves it back to that fund's checkbox once the new preview is ready. If you moved the focus while the import, the save or the new preview ran, such as to Wealthfolio's own menu, it stays there. Before, the focus fell to the page, so a keyboard or screen reader user had to find their place again from the top
- Keep a holding's units that a left-out folio, demat account or PRAN may hold, in a holdings import. When an earlier import of a left-out one recorded adding a holding that the import would change, the holding's row now says so and names it. A row whose statement lines would replace the account's units is left out by default, so the account keeps them unless you import it. A holding that the included folios, demat accounts and PRANs no longer list is kept instead of removed. An old asset isn't offered for replacement by the asset its fund is now confirmed to, and a fund left out for overlap doesn't offer to remove the account's units, since they may include the left-out one's. A holding that only a left-out one added stays as before. Before, the included lines replaced the account's whole position, without a note: with demat accounts 1 and 2 going to one account that held 150 shares, 100 from account 1 and 50 from account 2, leaving out account 2 brought the account down to 100
- Tell screen readers how a fund search, a preview, a check or a save is going, and when it's done. Each fund's section on the Funds and Holdings cards reads out "Searching…", then the number of results, "Nothing found…", the note that the ISIN found nothing or isn't printed and the name was searched instead, or "The search failed: …". A section reads these out once the focus has been in its search box or Search button, where Change and Choose a fund put it, so reading a statement doesn't read out the search every section starts by itself. A hidden status line reads out "Preparing the preview…", "Importing…", "Saving…" or "Checking the units…" while the page waits, then "The preview is ready: 13 new transactions, 0 already in Wealthfolio, 0 possible duplicates and 0 Wealthfolio can't import.", "The preview is ready: 2 snapshots." or "Compared the units of 2 of 6 holdings." Before, none of these had a live region, and the spinners in Search, Preview the import, Preview the snapshots and Check units were hidden from screen readers, so only alerts, such as a preview's warnings or an error, were read out while these ran or when they finished
- Ask only once whether to import a holding that a left-out folio, demat account or PRAN's earlier import recorded adding, in a holdings import. Once you import its row or remove the holding, the left-out one's import record no longer lists it, so the next import takes the statement's units by default, with no note. Its record of holdings this import keeps, such as one only it added, stays, and so do its records at other dates. A folio that shares one record with an included folio, linked through the issuer's folio number, still lists what that one imported. Before, its record still listed the holding, so each later import left the row out by default again with the same note, and saving without ticking Import kept the account's earlier units instead of the statement's new ones
- Notice a split recorded since an older holdings snapshot on the split's day in Wealthfolio's timezone setting, as Wealthfolio counts it, from the snapshot's date to the statement's. Before, a holdings import went by the split's UTC day, from the snapshot's date to the day after the statement's. With the timezone set to Asia/Kolkata, a split entered from 00:00 to 05:29 on the snapshot's date was missed: a holding the statement lists without a cost got Wealthfolio's pre-split average cost times the statement's units, so a 1:2 split of 10 shares that cost ₹1,000 proposed ₹2,000 for the 20 shares, and a holding carried over kept its pre-split units without the note to correct them. A split on the day after the statement's date, or before the snapshot's, could be flagged instead, which left out Wealthfolio's cost and asked to correct units that were right. A split whose date is missing or can't be read, such as 2025-02-30 or a time without its UTC offset, is flagged
- Give the keyboard focus back to the control that started a read, preview, check or import once it's done, if the focus fell to the page while the page was locked: Read statement, or PDF password when Enter there started the read, Preview the import, Preview the snapshots and Check units, whether they succeed or fail, and Import N activities when the import fails and its preview stays. A preview from Import this fund anyway that fails moves the focus to its error, "Couldn't prepare the preview: …", since the preview and its checkbox are gone. If you moved the focus while the page was locked, such as to Wealthfolio's own menu, it stays there. Before, the focus stayed on the page, so a keyboard or screen reader user had to find their place again from the top
- Match an earlier holdings import's record of a folio, demat account or PRAN to the same asset after its symbol or exchange changes in Wealthfolio, if the holding's remembered choice is that asset, confirmed while it had the recorded symbol and exchange. The preview then applies the existing snapshot rules using the asset's current symbol and exchange. So a statement of that folio, demat account or PRAN that no longer lists the holding can remove it by default, with the note "Not on the statement any more", even the first statement after the change. A holding now confirmed to another asset doesn't take over the old asset's record. Without such a choice, as when the holding was confirmed again or left out on the Holdings card since the change, or when the search result it was confirmed from gave another symbol or exchange than the asset's profile, the preview may treat the asset like a holding no import added, and saving a kept holding can drop its record as before. Before, leaving out the statement row of a renamed asset kept its units but dropped the folio's record of it, so the next statement that no longer listed the holding kept it by default, without the note

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- Import summary CAMS/KFintech statements and NSDL/CDSL depository statements as holdings snapshots: choose an account that tracks holdings for each folio or demat account (one chosen before that no longer tracks holdings is ignored, and its row says why to choose again), confirm each fund and share by its ISIN, and preview each account's snapshot. Holdings the statement doesn't list and cash carry over from the account's latest snapshot, except holdings an earlier import of the same folio or demat account added and the statement no longer lists; a folio's fund that an account tracking transactions already holds is left out by default; costs come from the statement, the account's average cost in INR, or what you type. A split recorded since the snapshot being carried over is flagged
- Record a fund's cash as transfers from and to a bank account Wealthfolio tracks, chosen for each PAN or folio: purchases, redemptions and IDCW payouts come with a transfer pair Wealthfolio links instead of a deposit or withdrawal, while switches and mergers keep theirs. The preview offers a deposit or withdrawal the bank account already has, within 7 days and for the same amount, in place of a new transfer; if you tick it, the add-on changes it into a transfer and links it. Transactions imported earlier with a deposit or withdrawal count as already in Wealthfolio
- Import bonds and NPS holdings from NSDL/CDSL statements. Each is searched first; when no result fits, "Use the statement's prices" creates a Wealthfolio asset priced by hand: a bond by its ISIN, an NPS scheme by a symbol from its printed name, one per scheme however many PRANs hold it. Each PRAN gets its own account choice, like a folio or demat account. A bond's proposed cost is its face value times the number of bonds, after the account's average cost. The printed details stay on the asset's metadata (`casStatement`, and Wealthfolio's own bond fields it doesn't have yet), so a data source can take over its prices later. An existing asset Wealthfolio prices from market data stays so
- Price summary and depository statements' funds and shares from the statement when no search result fits, like bonds and NPS schemes: "Use the statement's prices" creates an INR asset priced by hand, with the printed ISIN as its symbol, or for a summary fund without one, its registrar and scheme code (`MF-KFINTECH-TONDG`). Its printed name, ISIN, registrar and scheme code stay on the asset's metadata, and later holdings imports reuse it. Detailed statements search for such a fund again instead
- Check a summary or depository statement against accounts that track transactions, without an account that tracks holdings and without saving anything: Check units compares each confirmed holding's units, summed over its folios, demat accounts and PRANs, with what those accounts' posted activities add up to on the statement's date, and lists it as Match, Differ or Needs review.
