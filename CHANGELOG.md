### Added
- Propose a new Wealthfolio account under **Save to** when Wealthfolio has none a statement can go to, and create it only when the import is saved, so a first import into a new profile finishes in the add-on: "New: Mutual funds" for a detailed statement and a CAMS or KFintech summary statement, and "New: CDSL Demat" or "New: NSDL Demat" for a depository statement, preselected for the investor, each PAN and each folio printed without one. Under the select, "New · Wealthfolio account, created when you save · **Rename**" edits the name in place, 2 to 50 characters once trimmed, with an error tied to the field for anything else; saving waits while it's open. The bar at the bottom names the account it creates ("Create the Wealthfolio account **CDSL Demat** and save 10 holdings on 30 Jun 2026."), and a detailed import's button says "Create account and import 128 transactions". The account is a securities account in rupees, not the default, active, with no provider link, that tracks holdings for a summary or depository statement and transactions for a detailed one. It's created before anything is saved in it and remembered once it exists, so the next statement preselects it; nothing is remembered for one Wealthfolio doesn't create. Then nothing that needs it is saved and everything else is, and **Try again** creates only the accounts still missing and saves into those already created, never creating a second; a transaction that needs a missing account waits whole, with both sides of a gift between two investors' accounts and a trade's bank transfer. Wealthfolio checks a new account's transactions once it's created, just before they're imported, and one it refuses stays out with its reason. With accounts that fit but none chosen before, nothing is preselected and choosing where to save stays a question, with the new account in the list; a remembered account that's hidden, archived or tracks the wrong thing still asks you to choose again. Before, a first import into a new profile stopped at "No account tracks holdings. To save these holdings, add one in Wealthfolio, set to track holdings, then read the statement again." (or the same for transactions), leaving you to find where Wealthfolio sets an account's tracking mode and read the statement again. The add-on asks for one more permission, `create` among the accounts functions, so updating to this version needs approving a reinstall in Wealthfolio

## [3.0.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added
- Send each transaction's stamp duty, STT and TDS to Wealthfolio as tax lines labelled "Stamp duty", "STT" and "TDS", which add up to its tax. Wealthfolio keeps them with the activity and lists them under the tax in its details; they change no figure. Activities imported earlier keep only the total: importing the statement again doesn't add the lines
- Give each activity a detailed statement imports an ID made from its statement row, which Wealthfolio 3.13.0 keeps: a hash of the folio number, the date, the units and the amount, numbered when equal transaction rows repeat; charge and unsupported rows don't advance the count. The fund isn't part of it, since one statement can omit a fund's ISIN or print another registrar code than another. The trade, its cash and the bank account's side each have their own. A later import finds each row by its ID in the same account, even after the activity is edited, so equal transactions in one account each go in once: the same purchase on one day in two folios, two equal purchases on one day in one folio, or two funds' purchases whose deposits are equal. Before, the second was a possible duplicate that needed "Import anyway", and Wealthfolio couldn't tell the forced copy apart afterwards. Activities imported by version 2.2.0 or earlier, or entered by hand, are still found by their values, each for one row, so importing an earlier statement again adds nothing twice; Wealthfolio can still report an equal row as a duplicate of one of them, and "Import anyway" imports it with its ID. Each activity's metadata keeps the folio number, the registrar, casparser's type for the row and the statement's description, with anything shaped like a PAN replaced by `[PAN]`

### Changed
- Require Wealthfolio 3.13.0 or newer, which keeps the statement's name of a fund the import adds when the fund's Yahoo listing has no name ([wealthfolio/wealthfolio#2265](https://github.com/wealthfolio/wealthfolio/issues/2265)). Before, once Wealthfolio fetched the fund's profile after the import, it renamed the fund to its Yahoo ticker. A fund whose profile has a name still takes that name. Older versions refuse to install this version; Wealthfolio 3.12.0's desktop app does so without a message
- Count Wealthfolio's import check of a trade in a new fund chosen from AMFI like any other row's: an error keeps the transaction out, and the preview shows it. Wealthfolio 3.13.0's check accepts such a fund without an exchange ([wealthfolio/wealthfolio#2461](https://github.com/wealthfolio/wealthfolio/issues/2461)). Before, the add-on ignored a symbol error for such a trade, the one Wealthfolio 3.12.0's check gave each of them, and offered the transaction as new

### Fixed
- Show, search for and name a fund in a CDSL statement's CDSL demat account by its scheme name, without the fund house's company name and "#" that the statement prints before it: "FICTIVA ASSET MANAGEMENT PVT LTD # FICTIVA BALANCED ADVANTAGE FUND - DIRECT PLAN GROWTH" now reads "FICTIVA BALANCED ADVANTAGE FUND - DIRECT PLAN GROWTH" on the Holdings card, in the Left out alert and in Check units. When its ISIN finds nothing, the search now looks for "FICTIVA BALANCED ADVANTAGE"; before, it kept the company name and "#", which can leave Yahoo's search with no results. "Use the statement's prices" names a new asset, and its row in the preview, the same way. The asset's printed details keep the name as printed, and an asset an earlier import created keeps its name. Funds in an NSDL demat account within a CDSL statement, and in other statements, keep their names as printed
- Read the plan and option of a fund in a CDSL statement's CDSL demat account whose name CDSL cut short partway through a word, as in "… - DIRECT GROW". A name 100 to 105 characters long can be cut. Its last word now reads as the plan, option or other plan and option word it can only be the start of: "GROW" as Growth, so the "DIRECT" before it reads as the Direct plan too. That word counts when it follows a dash or another such word, so a cut in the fund's own name, like "FICTIVA REGU" for Fictiva Regular Savings Fund, stays unread. A word that could start words that read differently, like "D" (Direct, Dividend or Daily) or "RE" (Regular or Reinvestment), or a single letter outside a phrase, is still left unread. Before, such a name had no plan or option badge, so a search result or confirmed fund on another plan or option had no warning badge either. - Holdings imports find the account's own holding of an asset, other accounts' holdings of it and its pricing mode after its display code changes in Wealthfolio: they go by the symbol and exchange on each holding's asset profile. Before, a renamed holding confirmed without an asset ID was planned as a new one next to the account's existing holding, without its average cost; a folio's fund that an account tracking transactions holds wasn't left out by default, so importing it counted it twice; and a renamed asset's pricing mode, or its ID after saving, could be missed, leaving its statement price or details unsaved. A profile that can't be read stops the preview
- Check units on summary and depository statements, and a detailed statement's opening and closing units, count each activity on its day in Wealthfolio's timezone setting, as Wealthfolio does, or in UTC if none is set or it is invalid. Wealthfolio saves an activity entered in its form at the time of day entered, so its UTC day can differ: before, one entered at 02:00 in India on the day after a statement's date counted towards it, and one entered at 21:00 in Toronto on that date didn't
- Search for a fund in a CDSL statement's mutual fund folios by its scheme name, and name a new asset priced from the statement the same way, instead of using the scheme code printed before the name ("FMCDG - Fictiva Midcap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth" searched for "FMCDG"). The asset's printed details keep the name as printed
